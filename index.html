About

Background

I'm Smail Lotmani, an electronics and embedded-systems engineer. Over the past 15+ years I've designed control circuits, written firmware in C and assembly, laid out PCBs, and shipped products that went into mass production.

My work spans Microchip PIC microcontrollers, automotive CAN buses, medical devices, and robotics - including an autonomous robot that placed 2nd in the Algerian national Eurobot competition. More recently I've been building Vertex, a programming language and IDE inspired by Delphi and C++.
