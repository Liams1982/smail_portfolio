About

Professional Summary

Embedded Systems and Firmware Engineer with over 13 years of experience developing bare-metal C firmware for field-deployed electronic products. From PIC, ARM, and STM32 microcontroller programming to real-time sensor and motor control integration.

Strong hardware fluency across the full product lifecycle: schematic capture, PCB design (Eagle, Proteus, DesignSpark, KiCad), and SMT assembly. Domain experience spans robotics, medical devices (Class IV lasers), automotive electronics, and industrial automation. Founder of an electronics and robotics engineering practice.

International robotics competitor at Eurobot: 2nd place in Algeria and 32nd at the World Cup. Systems engineer by essence. I own every layer of the product, from concept to production to field support.

“I design the electronics, write the firmware, build the prototypes, support the production line, and debug it in the field, because that is the only way I can guarantee the product works.”
